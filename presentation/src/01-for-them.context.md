# Wouldn't it be nice — card text, long form

Not a slide. The exact card text before it was shortened. The deck slide is `01-for-them.md`.

## Engineering Efficency

Imagine a team is no longer completely blocked from production data. People are scoped to what their role allows, so they can contribute, grow, and become proficient, and the team is more effective, efficient, and robust.

## Perform Critical actions, without data leaks

Imagine a manual refund, or any action that would leak sensitive data. With role-based access we can build that action so it runs without revealing the data: the person with the role issues the manual refund, and no one sees the seed.

## Hardened Security

Imagine your laptop is compromised. With role-based access in place, defense in depth contains the damage a hacker can do, and the audit of the actions shows what the actor was trying to do.

## Military grade, inside crypto

Imagine role-based access is how confidential data is handled. Our own people cannot touch it, and every touch is logged. Which is how we stay ahead when competitors copy confidential intents. We define the standard.
