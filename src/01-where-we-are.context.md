# Where we are — card text, long form

Not a slide. The exact card text before it was shortened. The deck slide is `01-where-we-are.md`.

## It waits on a handful of people

Bug fixes, debugging, research, and deploys wait on a few people, mainly Anton. A one-line error sat for days on a wrong out-of-memory theory, because the log waited on someone who already had access. We learn who cannot act in the outage, including when that person is asleep.

## No role, so only the tenured learn it

Access is per person, with no expiry. Someone who changes team keeps what they had. Only the longest-tenured touch production, so only they learn it, and a 3am fix exists at 10am only as memory.

## The procedure is the exposure

A refund prints the seed in a pull request, and a person derives the key by hand. One confidential swap is explained by opening every user's data, or the partner gets no answer. Both legs of that swap exist together only in our logs.

## A service is open, or it is shut

A lead's laptop takes everything, and afterwards we cannot say what was read or who to warn. There is no classification. Confidential lines shut a whole service, legal exposure is not separated from what an attacker could do, and logs never expire.

## Under the cards

Only the tenured grow into production. The work relies on a few people, and there is no good path out. This is the infrastructure that gives us that path.
