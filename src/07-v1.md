## In one case {#v1--case .concept}

<p class="depth">A little more</p>

- A basic role model, applied to each service. The service gets one tier. People use it while the fuller design is still ahead.
- Vlad joins the bridge team on Monday. His lead adds him to bridge engineer. He reads tier 2 and tier 3 that day. He does not ask Anton. Tier 0 and tier 1 stay closed until a grant expires.
- The known compromise: a service is labelled as a whole. Eighty percent ordinary and twenty percent confidential means the ordinary part stays closed, or the confidential part leaks. poa-bridge-manager is the case. One line logs a key-lifecycle event, and the rest is ordinary.
- Version 2 is where sensitivity is defined, and the label moves onto the line. The roles survive that move.
- Two other ways of starting were set down. Labelling every line in all thirty services first blocks the first engineer. Doing that fully in one service leaves everyone else where they are. This hybrid is the third path.
