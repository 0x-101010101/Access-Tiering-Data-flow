## Beside the backdoor {#v4--beside .concept}

<p class="depth">A little more</p>

- A session through the gateway is recorded. What was typed is separate from what came back. A reviewer sees the commands, and asks for the output only when the commands warrant it.
- A session on the backdoor is not recorded. That is why the backdoor is for the test, and not the steady state.
- People use the gateway. When it fails, they use the path they already have. A fire is not blocked while the gateway is still being proven.
- Which product the gateway is, bought or built, is not decided here. The roles are the same roles.
