## Beside the direct path {#v4--beside .concept}

<p class="depth">A little more</p>

- A session through the bastion is recorded. What was typed is separate from what came back. A reviewer sees the commands, and asks for the output only when the commands warrant it.
- A session on the direct path is not recorded. That is why the direct path is for the test, and not the steady state. Break-glass makes it a recorded exception: a reason, a second person told, a hard stop.
- People use the bastion. When it fails, they use the path they already have. A fire is not blocked while the bastion is still being proven.
- Which product the bastion is, bought or built, is not decided here. The roles are the same roles.
