## On one service {#v2--case .concept}

<p class="depth">A little more</p>

- The label moves from the whole service onto the line. It lands one service at a time. A service that is fine with the version 1 label stays there.
- poa-bridge-manager is the case. One line logs a key-lifecycle event, and the rest is ordinary. Once it has moved, the ordinary lines can be read and the sensitive fields are absent.
- A line that has not been labelled keeps the service's version 1 label. Done, for this version, is that service.
- From here the record can name which fields sat above the role. It does not keep the rows.
- This is where the company starts following the data through Intents. Legal exposure and financial exposure are two kinds. They are not a fifth level.
